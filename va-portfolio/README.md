### How to Customize
1. Open index.html — replace all [Your Name] with your real name
2. Update bio text in the About section
3. Replace photo placeholder with your actual photo
4. Swap sample work cards with your real project screenshots
5. Update email, links, and platforms in the Contact section
6. To change brand colors, edit the :root variables in css/styles.css

### Hiding a Niche Portfolio Section
- In the browser: click the toggle pills in the Portfolio section
- Permanently: delete the <div id="sec-bk"> or <div id="sec-smm"> block from index.html
