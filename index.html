About Me

The Accountant Who Became a VA On Purpose

Before stepping into the world of virtual assistance, I spent four and a half years as a Multi-Country Tax Accountant, supporting clients across Australia, New Zealand, Singapore, and the Philippines. My background spans corporate and personal tax compliance, bookkeeping, financial reporting, and the behind-the-scenes systems that keep businesses running smoothly. In simple terms, I built a career around accuracy, organization, and solving problems before they became bigger ones.

My move into virtual assistance wasn't a sudden career change — it was a deliberate decision. I wanted to expand my skill set, explore the growing digital space, and build a career that offered both professional growth and greater flexibility to be present for the people who matter most to me.

So before I ever worked with a client, I became one of my own. I invested months learning website design, course creation platforms, social media management, and the tools that modern online businesses rely on. I built projects, practiced workflows, and created my portfolio through hands-on experience, not because a client required it, but because I believed preparation comes before opportunity.

Today, I help entrepreneurs, coaches, and business owners bring their ideas online through website and course creation, GoHighLevel CRM setup and business automation, while also offering bookkeeping and social media support. Although I'm relatively new to the virtual assistance industry, I bring something that can't be learned from a course alone: years of professional discipline, accountability, and attention to detail developed in a corporate environment.

My philosophy is simple: learn the tools, understand the process, and deliver work I can stand behind. No shortcuts, no overpromising, just reliable support that helps businesses grow.
